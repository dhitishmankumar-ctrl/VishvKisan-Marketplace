# Licence Guidance

This prototype is supplied without an asserted open-source licence. Until the project owner chooses a licence, all rights remain with the applicable copyright holder; do not assume others may reuse the code, logo or brand assets.

Before publishing:
1. Choose a licence that matches your goals (for example, MIT for permissive code reuse, or Apache-2.0 if you want an explicit patent grant). Seek advice if unsure.
2. Add the complete licence text and copyright notice.
3. Audit all images, fonts, icons, logos and other assets. Do not use government logos, farmer photographs or third-party artwork without permission or a suitable licence.
4. Treat the VishvKisan name/logo as a separate brand asset; code licensing does not automatically grant trademark rights.
5. Add attribution and notices required by any third-party libraries or services.
6. Publish a privacy policy and terms before collecting user data.
