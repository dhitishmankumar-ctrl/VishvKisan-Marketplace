# Sample Data Notes

## Market cards
The interface currently uses these illustrative examples only: Tomatoes ₹17/kg, Potatoes ₹23/kg, Leafy greens ₹56/head, Carrots ₹25/kg, Bananas ₹32/dozen, Apples ₹90/kg. These are not current market quotations and must not be used for trading decisions. Grain cards direct users to check official/local quotes.

## Community seed posts
The first three community posts are fictional demonstration content. New posts and likes are saved only in the current browser's localStorage and are not shared with other users.

## Scheme cards
Scheme summaries are brief discovery guides, not complete policy statements. The linked official portal is the source of truth. Review current scheme rules, state applicability and eligibility before publishing.

## Weather
Weather is not sample data when a request succeeds: it is fetched at runtime from Open-Meteo. The interface displays a failure message when the live request cannot be completed.
